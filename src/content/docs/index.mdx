<Accordion
  question="How does the Voices harmonizer work?"
  answer="The Voices section adds up to four pitch-shifted voices to your guitar signal. Each voice has independent control over: interval (±12 semitones), fine-tuning (cents), level, stereo pan, and delay. Scale quantization keeps harmonies in key with 8 scale types. MIDI mode allows real-time chord-following harmonies where your guitar provides the tone and MIDI controls the pitch."

/>

  question="What are the three amplifier models?"

(2) Lumina—crystal cleans with maximum headroom for jazz and funk. (3) Noctis—tight bass and aggressive high-gain for metal. Each has full tone controls (gain, bass, mid, treble, resonance, output) and saves settings independently."
  type="setting"
/>
<Accordion

/>
